## The challenge

The SIMOP project needed precise user stories. Writing them by hand consumed hours the team needed for decisions.

## The solution

We used generative AI to draft the user stories from project information, with human review on every delivery. I also designed Power BI dashboards so leadership could track progress.

## Result

The team's operational load dropped by 66%.
