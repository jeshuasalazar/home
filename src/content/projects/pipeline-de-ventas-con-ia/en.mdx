## The challenge

Every event generated many conversations on social media. Answering, qualifying and following up by hand took most of the team's time.

## The solution

We designed a Social Commerce strategy and automated the pipeline with AI: lead capture, qualification and follow-up, with a person in charge of the important decisions.

## Result

Up to 80% less time spent managing leads, and full events at venues such as CANACO and AMCP.
