## The challenge

An education platform needs the same things a large company does: payments, content, video, support and security. We are three people.

## The solution

Instead of hiring a full technical team, we designed a team of agents with clear roles. Agents write and review code, prepare content and help produce lessons; people set the direction and review what matters.

## What we learned

- An agent performs when it has a role, limits and a human owner.
- Security is designed from day one, not at the end.
- Speed comes from delegating what is repeatable, not from working longer hours.
