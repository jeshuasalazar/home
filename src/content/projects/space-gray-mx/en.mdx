## The challenge

In the used-Mac market nobody trusts anybody. Buyers don't know if a device is locked or if the battery and memory are what the listing says; sellers don't know if they'll get paid.

## The solution

Space Gray MX stands in the middle: it checks that each unit matches its listing and hands it over in a safe public place. I built the platform and the business model from scratch.

## Result

Sales grew 30x in 3 months, with 20+ units sold, and the business raised private capital.
