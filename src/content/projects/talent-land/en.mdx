## The challenge

Talent Land brings thousands of people together around technology. The challenge was turning that energy into community and real learning.

## What I did

For three editions I was community leader and ambassador: I rallied people, supported them and spoke in front of audiences of more than 2,000 people.

## What it gave me

I learned to explain technology simply to any audience. Today it's the foundation of my talks and of aiLearning.
